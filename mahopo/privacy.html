Mahopo Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 1, 2026

Kimy ("we") operates the mobile game Mahopo (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes, why, and your rights.

B-1. No sign-up and no server storage

The Game never asks for your name, email or phone number and does not create an account. Your saved adventure, unlock points, codex and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them too.

B-2. Information we process

Category Information Purpose How
Purchases Product ID, transaction ID and receipt data, purchase time Verify and deliver purchases ("Remove Ads"), restore purchases When you buy. Payment is handled by Google or Apple; we never receive card or payment details
Advertising Advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, IDFA only if you allow tracking), device model, OS, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad impressions, clicks and views Show ads, grant rewards for rewarded ads, cap ad frequency, personalized ads (with consent), ad fraud prevention Collected by ad SDKs (B-4)
Support Email address, your message Answer you, handle disputes When you email us
  • We do not process sensitive data, precise location, contacts or photos.
  • The Game contains no analytics or crash reporting tools.

B-3. Retention

Information Kept
Game records on your device Until you delete the app or its data (we never receive them)
Purchase records As required by law (in Korea, 5 years for payment and supply records, 3 years for complaint and dispute records), including records kept by the stores
Support emails 3 years after our reply
Data collected by ad partners Under each partner's policy (B-4)

B-4. Advertising

The Game is free and supported by ads, shown by the partner below through its SDK, which sends the "Advertising" data in B-2 to the partner's servers (United States and other countries).

Ad partner Data Purpose Policy
Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA) Advertising ID, device info, IP and approximate location, ad interactions Ad delivery, measurement, personalization, fraud prevention https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Opt out of personalized ads: on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS, choose "Ask App Not to Track" when asked, or turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You will still see ads, but not personalized ones.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before the first ad and can change it at any time under "Ad privacy" in the Game settings.
  • All forced ads (an occasional full-screen ad after a run ends) can be removed with the "Remove Ads" purchase. Rewarded ads play only when you tap them.

B-5. Your rights

  • Your game records live only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them.
  • Data held by the ad partner can be managed as described in B-4 (reset or delete the advertising ID, opt out of tracking).
  • To access, correct, delete or restrict any other information we hold (such as support emails), email dnjsgur2634@gmail.com. We reply within 10 days. Purchase records are kept for the legal periods in B-3.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell personal information.

B-6. Children

The Game is made for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the age set by your local law). Parents can contact dnjsgur2634@gmail.com.

B-7. Security and changes

Ad and store purchase traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not receive or store your game records. We will announce changes in the Game or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that reduce your rights).

Contact: Kimy (sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea) · Privacy officer: Owner of Kimy · dnjsgur2634@gmail.com

Mahopo(마호포) 개인정보처리방침

A-1. 회원가입도, 서버 저장도 없는 게임

게임은 이름, 이메일, 전화번호를 묻지 않고 계정을 만들지 않습니다. 모험 저장, 해금 점수, 도감, 설정은 모두 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장되고 회사로 보내지 않습니다. 앱을 지우면 이 정보도 함께 지워집니다.
