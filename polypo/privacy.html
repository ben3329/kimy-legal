Polypo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Kimy ("we") operates the mobile game Polypo (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes, why, how long we keep it, and your rights.

B-1. A game account without sign-up

The Game never asks for your name, email or phone number. On first launch it creates a game account identified by a random number and saves your progress to it. You may connect Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) to keep your progress when you change devices.

B-2. Information we process

Category Information Purpose How
Game account Game account ID (random), sign-in records, IP address Create and sign in to your account, prevent abuse Automatically at launch
Platform link (optional) Google Play Games or Game Center player ID Continue on a new device, leaderboards When you connect
Game progress Levels cleared, coins and items, Sea Book, settings, purchase status (e.g. ad removal), save time Cloud save and restore Automatically while playing
Daily Puzzle Date, number of tries, the moves of the winning try, result score, streak Verify results, calculate your worldwide top %, streak When you clear the Daily Puzzle
Leaderboards (optional) Streak, levels cleared Show Google Play Games / Game Center leaderboards Only if connected. Names and photos on leaderboards are managed by Google or Apple
Purchases Product ID, transaction ID and receipt data, purchase time Verify and deliver purchases, restore purchases, handle refunds When you buy. Card and payment details are handled by Google or Apple; we never receive them
Advertising Advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, IDFA only if you allow tracking), device model, OS, language, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad impressions, clicks and views Show ads, grant rewards for rewarded ads, cap ad frequency, personalized ads (with consent), ad fraud prevention Collected by ad SDKs (B-5)
Support Email address, your message, game account ID you give us Answer you, handle disputes When you email us
  • Stays on your device: language choice, a copy of your sound and vibration settings, and in-level state are stored only on your device.
  • Share cards are drawn on your device and handed to your system share sheet. We do not receive the image or who you send it to. Cards contain no name or account ID.
  • Daily Puzzle ranking shows no names. We only count results per day to calculate percentages.
  • We do not process sensitive data, precise location, contacts or photos.

Data is transferred over encrypted connections whenever you use online features. If you do not want this, you can stop using the Game and delete your account (B-6); cloud save and the Daily Puzzle will then be unavailable. Google and Apple process data under your agreements with them.

B-5. Advertising

The Game is free and supported by ads, shown by the partners below through their SDKs.

Ad partner Data Purpose Policy
Google AdMob (Google LLC) Advertising ID, device info, IP and approximate location, ad interactions Ad delivery, measurement, personalization, fraud prevention https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Opt out of personalized ads: on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS, choose "Ask App Not to Track" when asked, or turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You will still see ads, but not personalized ones.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before the first ad and can change it at any time in the Game settings.
  • We never pass your game account ID, progress or Daily Puzzle records to ad partners.
  • All forced ads can be removed with the "Remove Ads" purchase. Rewarded ads play only when you tap them.

B-6. Your rights and account deletion

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your information at any time.

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your game account, progress and Daily Puzzle records on our servers are deleted immediately and cannot be restored. Purchased items and coins are lost (purchase records are kept only for the legal periods in B-3).
  • On the web: if you no longer have the app, follow the account deletion guide and email dnjsgur2634@gmail.com with the game account ID shown in the app settings. We act within 10 days of confirming the request.
  • Google Play Games / Game Center links and leaderboard entries can be removed in each platform's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell personal information.

B-7. Children

The Game is made for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the age set by your local law). If we learn that we have, we will turn off personalized ads and delete the account. Parents can contact dnjsgur2634@gmail.com.

B-8. Security

All traffic between the app and servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Server access rules let players reach only their own data. Admin access is limited and admin keys are never stored in code or documents.

B-9. Changes and contact

We will announce changes in the Game or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that reduce your rights).
