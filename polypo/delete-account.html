Polypo account deletion

How to delete your Polypo game account and the data stored on our servers.

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Polypo.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) at the top right → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm once more. Your data is deleted right away.

No app? Request by email

  1. Email: dnjsgur2634@gmail.com
  2. Subject: Polypo account deletion
  3. Include your game account ID, shown in the app under Settings → Account. If you linked Google Play Games or Game Center, reinstall the app and sign in with the same account to see the same ID.
  4. We delete the data within 10 days of confirming your request and reply by email.

What is deleted

  • Your game account, progress (levels, coins, items, Sea Book, settings), purchase status, Daily Puzzle records and streak.
  • Deleted data, purchased items and coins cannot be restored.

What is kept

  • Purchase records, for the periods required by law (in Korea, 5 years for payment and supply records, 3 years for complaint and dispute records), then deleted.
  • Daily Puzzle totals per day (number of players per result), which are anonymous counts.
  • Google Play Games / Game Center leaderboard entries and links can be removed in each platform's settings.
  • Data collected by ad partners can be disconnected by deleting or resetting your device's advertising ID (Privacy Policy B-5).

Polypo(폴리포) 계정 삭제

Polypo 게임 계정과 서버에 저장된 데이터를 지우는 방법입니다.

앱에서 지우기 (바로 처리)

  1. Polypo를 엽니다.
  2. 오른쪽 위 톱니(설정) → 계정 → 계정 삭제를 누릅니다.
  3. 한 번 더 확인하면 바로 지워집니다.

앱이 없을 때: 메일로 요청

  1. 메일 받는 곳: dnjsgur2634@gmail.com
  2. 제목: Polypo 계정 삭제 요청
  3. 본문에 게임 계정 ID를 적어 주세요. 계정 ID는 앱의 설정 → 계정에 보입니다. Google Play 게임즈나 Game Center를 연결해 두었다면 앱을 다시 설치하고 같은 계정으로 들어가면 같은 ID가 보입니다.
